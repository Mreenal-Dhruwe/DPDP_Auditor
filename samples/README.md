## Reading the Juice Shop results

Juice Shop has no answer key, so this is our manual review of the 60 findings:

- **Real and serious (high confidence)**
  - `models/user.ts:73` stores passwords as unsalted MD5 via `security.hash()` (Juice Shop's own
    `weakPasswordChallenge`), plus 4 places in `routes/` that hash passwords the same way (SEC-003)
  - `data/static/users.yml`: 14 seed users with plaintext passwords (SEC-004)
  - committed private keys in `lib/insecurity.ts` and two Terraform files; a hard-coded
    `testingPassword` in the login component (SEC-004)
  - `Cards.cardNum` stored as a plain INTEGER (SEC-002)
  - `routes/changePassword.ts:56` sends the whole User record, password hash included, back
    to the browser after a password change (SEC-006)
  - access logs deleted after 2 days (`server.ts:368`, `max_logs: '2d'`) and CloudWatch logs after
    30 days (both Terraform setups), against Rule 8(3)'s one year (SEC-010)
- **Info**: both Terraform setups deploy to `eu-west-1`, outside India; allowed unless restricted (TP-002)
- **Real pattern, not app code (high confidence)**: 14 SEC-003 hits in `data/static/codefixes/` —
  Juice Shop's coding-challenge answer snippets, which repeat the vulnerable login code
- **Low confidence, by design**: 12 hard-coded JWTs / password hashes in test files; 4 RET-001
  findings (Juice Shop generates REST endpoints with finale-rest, so delete paths may exist
  outside the code)
- **Not found, and why**: SEC-005 reports nothing — Juice Shop's personal-data APIs are generated by
  finale-rest behind `security.isAuthorized()`, and its known IDORs (other users' baskets) are
  routes that *are* authenticated but skip an ownership check, which we do not analyse yet.
  SEC-001 reports nothing because Juice Shop's log calls carry no personal data. CH-001 and
  CH-002 do not apply: Juice Shop never asks for a date of birth or age.

## Reading the NodeGoat results

NodeGoat (Express + the raw MongoDB driver) has no answer key; our reading of the 23 findings:

- **Real:** plaintext `ssn`, `bankAcc`, `bankRouting` and `password` written with `users.update` /
  `insert` (SEC-002); hardcoded `cookieSecret`, `cryptoKey`, a committed private key and plaintext
  seed passwords (SEC-004); session cookie without `secure` (SEC-008); no erase route and no
  breach or grievance process (RET-001, RT-002, RT-003, BR-001, low confidence); no notice on the
  profile and signup forms (NOT-001, low confidence).
- **Lowered:** the ZAP API key in `config/env/development.js` and `test.js` (medium, development files).
- **Doubtful:** CH-001 (a date of birth on an adult profile, low confidence); SEC-008 on
  `http.createServer` (low confidence, TLS may end at a proxy); SEC-004 on
  `app/views/tutorial/a6.html:83`, an example snippet in a tutorial page.
- **Not found:** SEC-006 for the DAO results spread into templates (`profile.js`, `session.js`: the
  record comes from a DAO in another file), the IDOR on `/allocations/:userId`, a plaintext
  password comparison (not a hashing choice) and the end-of-life `node:12` image.
